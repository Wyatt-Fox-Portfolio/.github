Heirloom: AI family-history platform
  Live (private beta): heirloom-ee950.web.app · I'm the founder and CTO.

Families create a shared space where an AI voice interviewer talks with their relatives. Every conversation becomes part of a living family tree, timeline, and set of biographies, with each fact traced back to the words it came from.
- A real-time voice interviewer with custom turn-taking. A simulation harness brought its share of the conversation from 34% to 19% and its reply delay from about 4.5 s to 0.8 s.
- An LLM pipeline with versioned prompts, schema validation, and a human review queue for anything uncertain.
- Kinship derived from a two-edge family graph, and multi-family security built on short-lived credentials and membership rules.

MMA Intelligence: fight forecasting system
  A self-running system that rates professional fighters, prices upcoming UFC fights with machine-learning models, compares those prices with the betting market, and records paper bets, all on a schedule with no manual steps. Predictions are committed to git before each card, and they are scored only against results that independent sources agree on.
